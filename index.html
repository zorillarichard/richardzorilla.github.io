About me

I'm Richard Zorilla, a computer engineer with a strong foundation in programming and digital hardware. I work in C++, Java, MATLAB, PHP, and Verilog, and I build embedded systems and 3D designs in Fusion 360.

I'm most interested in the point where software meets hardware: designing circuits, programming microcontrollers, and taking existing devices apart to learn how they work. I keep improving by building hands-on projects.
